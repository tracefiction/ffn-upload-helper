# Privacy

The extension collects no data. It runs only on FanFiction.net's Doc Manager pages, sends your documents only to FanFiction.net, and stores its settings in your browser.

It also keeps a log of your last 10 uploads in your browser for the debug report. For each try it saves the time, FanFiction.net's reply and which of its servers answered. It also notes the file type and size, how many documents you have, and the Cloudflare location and country you connect through. It doesn't save the document, its label, the file name or your IP address. Nothing leaves your browser unless you copy the report and send it to someone.
